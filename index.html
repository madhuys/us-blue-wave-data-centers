Household power bills flipped the political map.

On November 3, 2026, voters delivered unified Democratic control of Congress (at least 255 House seats and 54 Senate seats as of November 13 tallies) while President Trump retains veto power. Statehouses and utility boards moved in lockstep: Democrats flipped six governorships across Texas (Gina Hinojosa), Georgia (Keisha Lance Bottoms), Ohio (Amy Acton), Nevada, Iowa, and Alaska, broke five Republican trifectas, and won a 3 to 2 majority on the Georgia Public Service Commission for the first time in decades.

Exit polls showed 76% of voters opposed local data centers and 56% called them a major factor in their vote.
